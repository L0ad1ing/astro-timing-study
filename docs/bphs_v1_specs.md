# BPHS vol. 1 — definitions as the book states them (G.C. Sharma tr., Sagar; PDF page = book page + 11)
Working notes while reading; each item cites the book page. These replace the engine's general-knowledge tables.

## Ch. 1-2 (pp. 1-11): creation; incarnations of the planets (Rama = Sun, Krishna = Moon, ...). No rules.

## Ch. 3 — planetary characters (pp. 12-)
- v. 4-6 (p. 13): 27 nakshatras from Ashwini; 12 signs from Aries; the rising sign = Lagna.
- v. 8-9 (p. 16): positions by drik-ganita (observation-matching calculation); Lagna from local rising times.
- **v. 11 (p. 16-17) benefic/malefic:** Sun, Saturn, Mars, decreasing (waning) Moon, Rahu, Ketu = malefic; full Moon,
  Mercury, Jupiter, Venus = benefic; **Mercury conjunct a malefic becomes malefic.** Notes: the Moon is malefic from
  Krishna 8th to Shukla 8th tithi, benefic from Shukla 8th to Krishna 8th; Mercury with both kinds = mixed.
  ENGINE CHANGE: natural benefic/malefic must be computed per chart (Moon by tithi, Mercury by conjunction), not fixed.
- v. 12-13 (p. 17): karakatwa - Sun soul, Moon mind, Mars strength, Mercury speech, Jupiter knowledge and happiness,
  Venus semen (potency), Saturn grief.
- v. 14-15 (p. 19): cabinet - Sun and Moon royal, Mars commander, Mercury prince, Jupiter and Venus ministers,
  Saturn servant, Rahu and Ketu the army.
- v. 16-17 (p. 20): complexions. v. 18 (p. 21): deities. v. 19 (p. 21): sex - Mercury and Saturn neuter, Moon and
  Venus female, Sun, Mars, Jupiter male.
- v. 20 (p. 23): elements - Mars fire, Mercury earth, Jupiter ether, Venus water, Saturn air (matches vol. 2 ch. 78).
- v. 21 (p. 24): castes - Jupiter, Venus Brahmin; Sun, Mars Kshatriya; Moon, Mercury Vaishya; Saturn Shudra.
- v. 22 (p. 26): Sun, Moon, Jupiter sattvic; Venus, Mercury rajasic; Mars, Saturn tamasic.
- v. 35-38 (p. 34): directional strength - Mercury, Jupiter east (1st); Sun, Mars south (10th); Saturn west (7th); Moon,
  Venus north (4th); Moon, Mars, Saturn strong at night, Mercury always, others by day; malefics strong in Krishna
  paksha, benefics in Shukla; benefics in Uttarayana; strength order of the period lords; natural strength ascending
  Saturn, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Moon, Sun.
- v. 49-54 (pp. 36-37): exaltation Sun Aries 10, Moon Taurus 3, Mars Capricorn 28, Mercury Virgo 15, Jupiter Cancer 5,
  Venus Pisces 27, Saturn Libra 20; debilitation the 7th sign same degree. Moolatrikona: Sun Leo 0-20 (rest own),
  Moon Taurus 3-30 (0-3 exaltation), Mars Aries 0-12, Mercury Virgo 15-20 (0-15 exaltation, 20-30 own), Jupiter
  Sagittarius 0-10, Venus Libra 0-15, Saturn Aquarius 0-20.
- v. 55 (pp. 37-39): natural friendship from the moolatrikona sign: lords of the 2, 4, 5, 8, 9, 12th and the exaltation
  lord are friends; table incl. Rahu (friends Jupiter, Venus, Saturn; enemies Sun, Moon, Mars; neutral Mercury) and
  Ketu (friends Mars, Venus, Saturn; enemies Sun, Moon; neutral Mercury, Jupiter) - "differences of opinion" noted.
- v. 56-58 (pp. 39-41): temporary friends in the 2, 3, 4, 10, 11, 12th; compound table (5-fold).
- v. 59-60 (p. 42): result shares exalted 1, moolatrikona 3/4, own 1/2, friendly 1/4, neutral 1/8, debilitated /
  combust / enemy 0; inauspicious results the reverse.
- v. 61-65 (pp. 42-43): Dhooma = Sun + 4s13d20m; Vyatipata = 12s - Dhooma; Parivesha = Vyatipata + 6s; Indrachapa =
  12s - Parivesha; Upaketu = Indrachapa + 16d40m (+ 1s = Sun, check). With the Sun, Moon or Lagna they destroy dynasty,
  longevity, wisdom.
- v. 66-70 (pp. 43-47): Gulika etc. from the day/night divided in 8 (Saturn's portion = Gulika; Sun's Kala, Mars's
  Mrityu, Jupiter's Yamaghantaka, Mercury's Ardhaprahara); night portions start from the 5th weekday lord.
- v. 71-74 (pp. 48-49): Pranapada from the time since sunrise (palas / 15 as signs, added to the Sun by its sign
  quality); in the 2, 4, 5, 9, 10, 11th from the Lagna an auspicious birth.
- NOT in ch. 3: combustion orbs; Rahu/Ketu exaltation.

## Ch. 4 — signs (pp. 50-60)
- v. 4: limbs - Aries head, Taurus face, Gemini arms, Cancer heart, Leo stomach, Virgo hip, Libra below the navel,
  Scorpio private parts, Sagittarius thighs, Capricorn knees, Aquarius ankles, Pisces feet.
- v. 5: movable (Ar Cn Li Cp), fixed (Ta Le Sc Aq), dual (Ge Vi Sg Pi); odd signs cruel/male, even benign/female;
  humours bile (Ar Le Sg), wind (Ta Vi Cp), mixed (Ge Li Aq), phlegm (Cn Sc Pi).
- v. 6-24: rising by the head - Gemini, Leo, Virgo, Libra, Scorpio, Aquarius; by the back - Aries, Taurus, Cancer,
  Sagittarius, Capricorn; both - Pisces. Strong at night: Aries, Taurus, Gemini, Cancer, Sagittarius, Capricorn; by day
  the others. Elements: Aries, Leo, Sagittarius fire; Taurus, Capricorn earth; Gemini, Virgo(? 'air' in the translation),
  Aquarius air; Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces water (Virgo's element as printed is air - logged).
- v. 25-30: Nisheka Lagna.
