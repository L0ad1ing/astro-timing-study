# Pre-registration — the author's BCP chain method (written before running)

*Public copy: personal details in the original (the author's own life events, used as context and as the
reason for the addendum) are redacted. Method, targets, statistics, pass bars and expectations are unchanged.*

Method (as described by the author, 2026-10-01):
- Active house for completed age a: ((a - 1) mod 12) + 1. It is the MAIN story.
- Follow links: a house -> its lord and the planets in it; a planet -> the house it sits in and the houses it rules.
  Steps from the active house rank importance: step 0 MAIN; steps 1-2 SECONDARY (lord, the house it sits in,
  its lordships); steps 3-4 FAINT; further = background. Score: MAIN 3, SECONDARY 2, FAINT 1, background 0.
- Cycle ruler per 12-year cycle (Moon, Mercury, Venus, Sun, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn): included in personal
  stories; NOT testable in the large test (constant within 12 years and identical to an age bracket).
Context: [redacted — two past life periods in the author's chart that the chain was said to fit].

Test 1 (personal, blind): stories for ages 18-44; about half show the real chain, the rest a decoy chain (an
active house with a different lord). The author scores each 0-3 without knowing which is which.
Pass: real-chain stories score higher on average than decoys (one-sided permutation p < 0.05).

Test 2 (Astro-Databank, all people; no fitting, so both halves are used): event year vs the same person's
years -3, -1, +1, +3 (own death excluded: past-only controls and age structure). Target houses fixed here:
Marriage 7; Divorce 7; Birth_Child 5; death of father 9, mother 4, spouse 7, child 5, sibling 3; Career_Peak 10;
Arrest 12 or 6; Illness 6; Accident 8; Prize 10 or 11; Job_Start 10; Job_End 10 or 12; Trial 6;
Relationship_Begin 7 or 5; Relationship_End 7. Needs a known birth time (Lagna).
Statistic: P(event-year score > control-year score), ties half; 0.5 = chance; bootstrap 95% CI. Also the share of
events whose target house is the MAIN story vs controls (chance ~ 1/12 for one target house).
Expectation (mine): 0.49-0.52 for most event types; no type above 0.55.

## Addendum (2026-10-01, before running): running-year convention
Some practitioners count the RUNNING year of life: active = (completed age mod 12) + 1 (the 41st year -> house 5).
Raised by a personal example [redacted] that falls in a different house under the two conventions.
Same targets, statistic and pass bar as Test 2. Expectation (mine): 0.49-0.52.
